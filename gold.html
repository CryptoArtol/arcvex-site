What Gold is

Earned from Silver. Paid in XRP.

You don't buy Gold — you earn it. Burn your Silver and mint Gold at a fixed rate. That's the only way Gold enters circulation, which keeps it scarce.

Holding Gold gives you a share of the reward pool: real XRP collected from platform activity, paid out to Gold holders on a regular schedule, pro-rata to how much Gold you hold. The more the ecosystem plays, the bigger the pool.
