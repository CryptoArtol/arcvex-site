Why rarity matters

Your rank decides your paycheck.

First, the supply: Silver is hard-capped at 589,000. It's minted once — never printed weekly. That fixed pool is then dripped to holders across 104 weekly drops (~2 years), weighted by rarity.

Every week, all 3,333 pets are ranked by their current traits — not how they minted. Your weekly Silver payout is weighted by that rank: the rarer you sit, the bigger your slice. Top pets pull up to 45× what the floor earns.

Rarity here is live and relative. Snapshot is taken Monday, Silver pays out Tuesday — then the board resets and the race starts again. Chase a rare trait and the crowd chases it too, until it's common and a different trait becomes the crown. Climb, hold your spot, and keep earning.

You don't have to play to earn — just holding pays Silver by rarity. But spinning, swapping and animating your pet to climb the ranks is how you turn a floor paycheck into a top one.
