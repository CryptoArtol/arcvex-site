What it's for

A spending token, not a holding token.

Bronze exists for one job: minting Big Pets. You acquire Bronze, then spend it to mint a pet from the collection — and because you're paying in Bronze, you get 20% off versus paying in raw XRP.

The supply is capped: there will only ever be 589,000,000 Bronze (589M), and that number never grows. Bronze is meant to be spent on NFTs — it flows into mints, not sat on. As the collection mints out, Bronze gets used up against pets.

Changed your mind before you mint? Bronze has a standing buyback — sell it back any time and you only ever give up the flat 20% spread. No trapped bags, no waiting for a buyer.

The tiered launch

Early tiers are cheapest. Bronze launches in price tiers. The opening tier is the lowest price it will ever be.

2

Prices step up as it sells. Each tier that fills pushes the next one higher — later mints cost more than the first ones. Simple: the longer you wait, the more you pay.

Mint with Bronze, save 20%. However much you paid for it, minting in Bronze still beats paying in XRP by 20%.
