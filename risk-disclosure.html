Arcvex is an automated trading tool. It is not a financial advisor, investment manager, or broker. Nothing on this platform constitutes financial advice. You should consult a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions.

Trading cryptocurrency involves substantial risk of loss. You should only trade with funds you can afford to lose entirely.

1. Market Risks

Volatility

Cryptocurrency markets are significantly more volatile than traditional financial markets. Prices can move 10%, 20%, or more in a single day. Automated trading bots execute your strategy regardless of market conditions — they do not "know" when to stop during extreme volatility unless you configure safeguards.

Liquidity risk

Some tokens and trading pairs have limited liquidity, particularly on decentralised exchanges. Low liquidity can result in significant slippage — the difference between your expected execution price and the actual price. DCA and Grid bots operating in low-liquidity markets may execute at substantially worse prices than anticipated.

Market manipulation

Cryptocurrency markets are subject to manipulation including pump-and-dump schemes, wash trading, and spoofing. Automated bots may execute trades based on manipulated price signals.

Correlation risk

During market-wide events, assets that normally move independently may all decline simultaneously. Diversifying across multiple bots or strategies does not eliminate this risk.

2. Technical Risks

Software errors

Despite rigorous testing, automated trading software may contain bugs that result in unintended trading behaviour, including executing orders at wrong prices, failing to execute orders, or executing more orders than intended.

Blockchain network issues

The blockchains Arcvex operates on (XRPL, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, Hyperliquid) may experience congestion, outages, forks, or upgrades that disrupt trading activity. Transactions may be delayed, fail, or execute at unexpected prices during network disruptions.

DEX risks

Decentralised exchanges may experience smart contract vulnerabilities, liquidity pool imbalances, oracle manipulation, or front-running (MEV extraction). These risks are outside Arcvex's control and can result in losses.

RPC and data feed failures

Arcvex relies on RPC nodes and data feeds to read blockchain state and submit transactions. If these services experience outages or return incorrect data, bot behaviour may be affected.

Internet and infrastructure

Trading bots require continuous internet connectivity and server uptime. While we maintain infrastructure designed for high availability, interruptions can and do occur.

3. Strategy Risks

No guaranteed returns

No trading strategy — whether manual or automated — guarantees profits. Past performance of any strategy, including historical backtest results, is not indicative of future performance.

Backtesting limitations

If backtesting features are available, results are based on historical data and hypothetical execution. Actual trading results will differ due to slippage, fees, liquidity changes, and market conditions that did not exist in the historical period.

Configuration errors

Bots execute exactly what you configure. Incorrect parameter settings — such as wrong price ranges, inappropriate order sizes, or unsuitable grid spacing — can result in significant losses. It is your responsibility to understand and correctly configure your trading strategy.

Changing market conditions

A strategy that performs well in ranging markets may perform poorly in trending markets, and vice versa. Market conditions change, and strategies that were profitable in the past may become unprofitable.

4. Regulatory and Legal Risks

Evolving regulation

The legal and regulatory landscape for cryptocurrency and automated trading is changing rapidly across jurisdictions. New laws or regulatory actions may restrict, prohibit, or impose additional requirements on automated trading, specific tokens, or cryptocurrency trading in general.

Tax obligations

Automated trading may generate a high volume of taxable events. You are solely responsible for understanding and complying with tax obligations in your jurisdiction. Arcvex does not provide tax advice.

Jurisdictional restrictions

Some jurisdictions may restrict or prohibit the use of automated trading tools or specific cryptocurrencies. It is your responsibility to ensure that your use of Arcvex complies with the laws of your jurisdiction.

5. Wallet and Custody Risks

Self-custody responsibility

Arcvex is non-custodial. You are solely responsible for the security of your wallet, private keys, and seed phrases. Lost keys cannot be recovered by Arcvex or anyone else.

Wallet connection risks

Connecting your wallet to the Arcvex dashboard grants permission for the Service to submit transactions on your behalf within your configured parameters. Ensure you understand the permissions you are granting.

Smart contract interaction

Bots interact with DEX smart contracts. These contracts may contain vulnerabilities that could result in loss of funds, even if the Arcvex software functions correctly.

6. Financial Risks

Loss of capital

You can lose some or all of the capital allocated to automated trading. Do not allocate funds you cannot afford to lose.

Fee impact

Trading fees (blockchain transaction fees, DEX fees, and subscription fees) reduce your returns. In low-volatility or low-spread environments, fees may exceed trading profits, resulting in a net loss even when the strategy "works."

Opportunity cost

Capital allocated to automated trading strategies is not available for other investments. Automated strategies may underperform simple buy-and-hold approaches in certain market conditions.

7. Counterparty and Platform Risks

DEX protocol risk

The decentralised exchanges your bots trade on are independent protocols. Protocol failures, governance attacks, or economic exploits on these platforms are outside our control.

Token risk

Individual tokens may lose all value due to project failure, rug pulls, regulatory action, or loss of community support. Automated bots will continue to trade these tokens unless you manually intervene.

Bridge risk

If using cross-chain features, bridged assets carry additional risk related to bridge security and solvency.
