The whole point

NFTs that don't sit still.

Most XRPL NFTs are frozen the second they're minted. Arcvex Lions are minted with the tfMutable flag set — so the art and traits can change after you own them. Two ways we can evolve a Lion:

Off-chain
re-skin		 We change what the token's URI serves — image and traits update live in every wallet. Free, instant, infinite. This is the reactive / "living art" layer. No transaction
On-chain
evolution		 A real NFTokenModify transaction (XLS-46) rewrites the on-chain URI itself — for permanent "evolution" events and milestones. One ledger tx

Questions

Good to know

What does "mutable" actually mean?

Each Lion is minted with the XRP Ledger's tfMutable flag. That lets the metadata behind the token change after mint — so the same NFT you hold can be re-skinned or evolved later, without us ever moving it out of your wallet.

Why your own launchpad instead of XRP.cafe?

Two reasons. First, control — minting with the mutable flag set, on our terms, with our branding. Second, no per-mint platform fees. You still get full XRPL liquidity because Lions can be listed on any XRPL marketplace for secondary sales.

Which wallet do I need?

Xaman (formerly XUMM). You sign in and approve the mint payment from your phone — we never see or hold your private keys.

How much does it cost to mint?

[Mint price TBA] in XRP, plus the negligible XRPL network fee (a fraction of a cent). Final price and launch date will be announced before mint opens.

Is it allowlist only?

The launchpad opens private/allowlist-first. Each allowlisted wallet gets a set number of mints. A public window may follow — see the roadmap.
